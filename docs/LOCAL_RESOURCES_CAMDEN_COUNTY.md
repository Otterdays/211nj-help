## 1. Municipal & County Government Centers

### Winslow Township Municipal Complex
The governing civic body for residents of Sicklerville (08081).
* **Address**: 125 South Route 73, Braddock, NJ 08037
* **Main Switchboard**: `609-567-0700`
* **Official Website**: [winslowtownship.com](https://www.winslowtownship.com)
* **Key Municipal Services**:
  - **Tax Collector / Assessor**: Property tax assessments, Senior Freeze certifications, and property tax deduction applications (Veterans and Senior/Disabled deductions).
  - **Community Development / Code Enforcement**: Zoning permits, local housing inspections, and emergency repair citations.
  - **Recreation Department**: Information on community events, Family Day school-supplies giveaways, and park facilities.

### Camden County Store at Voorhees Town Center
A convenient satellite center providing county services without traveling into downtown Camden.
* **Address**: Voorhees Town Center, 1114 Somerdale Rd, Voorhees, NJ 08043 (Upper Level)
* **Phone**: `856-566-2920`
* **Services**:
  - Passport applications and photos.
  - Camden County resident identification cards.
  - Camden County Veteran ID cards and benefits referrals.
  - Public transit schedules, county maps, and Senior & Disabled Services applications.

### Camden County Board of Social Services (BSS)
The primary administrative center for federal and state cash assistance, food, and Medicaid.
* **Main Headquarters**: 508 Market Street, Camden, NJ 08102
* **Telephone**: `856-225-8800`
* **Administered Programs**:
  - NJ SNAP (Food Stamps) intake, recertifications, and expedited claims.
  - Work First New Jersey (TANF cash assistance for families and GA for individuals).
  - NJ FamilyCare / Medicaid applications (Aged, Blind, Disabled, and Family Care).
  - Emergency Assistance (EA) for rent arrears, security deposits, and temporary shelter.

### Camden County Department of Children's Services
* **Address**: DiPiero Center, 512 Lakeland Road, Blackwood, NJ 08012
* **Telephone**: `856-374-6376` (Subsidy Unit: `609-989-8035`)
* **Role**: Administers child care subsidies (CCAP) for working parents and students throughout Camden County.

#### Museum Pass Borrowing Rules:
1. Passes are checked out on an **adult card** for **4 calendar days**.
2. **No renewals or reservations by phone**: First-come, first-served at the circulation desk.
3. **Late Fee**: **$5.00 per day** overdue.
4. **Replacement Liability**: If lost, the borrower is billed the full annual membership replacement cost of the pass (ranging from $150 to $500).
5. Must be returned to the **same branch** from which it was borrowed.

## 3. Emergency Screening, Mental Health & Crisis Centers

### Oaks Integrated Care Camden County Crisis Screening Center
The designated psychiatric emergency screening service (PESS) for Camden County.
* **Crisis Hotline (24/7)**: `856-428-4357` (856-428-HELP)
* **Hospital Location**: Jefferson Health Cherry Hill, 2201 Chapel Ave West, Cherry Hill, NJ 08002
* **Scope**: Acute crisis evaluation, mobile crisis screening team dispatches, and voluntary/involuntary inpatient stabilization referrals.

### PerformCare Children's System of Care (Youth Behavioral Crisis)
* **24/7 Dispatch**: `877-652-7624`
* **Mobile Response & Stabilization Services (MRSS)**:
  - Dispatches trained behavioral health clinicians to the home anywhere in Camden County within **1 hour**.
  - Provides de-escalation for youth experiencing behavioral meltdowns, aggression, or acute trauma without involving police or ERs.
  - Follow-up in-home stabilization for up to 8 weeks.

### State & National Crisis Lines
* **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: Dial or text `988` (24/7 free emotional support).
* **ReachNJ (Addiction / Substance Use)**: `1-844-732-2465` (1-844-REACHNJ).
* **NJ Mental Health Cares**: `1-866-202-4357` (Mon–Sun 8am–8pm).
* **Domestic Violence Hotline (Camden County Women's Center)**: `1-800-572-7233` (856-227-1234).

## 6. Inclusive Parks & Low-Sensory Recreation

### Jake's Place at New Brooklyn Park
* **Location**: New Brooklyn County Park, 442 New Brooklyn-Cedar Brook Rd, Sicklerville, NJ 08081
* **Highlights**:
  - A modern, fully all-inclusive, barrier-free playground engineered for children and adults of all physical and neurodevelopmental abilities.
  - Ramp access to all elevated play structures, specialized sensory activity boards, adaptive swings, and wide wheelchair-accessible pathways.
  - The surrounding county park features a peaceful pond, fishing docks, and a free 18-hole disc golf course.

### Frank Donio Memorial Park
* **Location**: 675 Chews Landing Rd, Sicklerville, NJ 08081
* **Highlights**: 50 acres of township parkland featuring walking paths, sports fields, modern playground equipment, and direct trail access into the quiet Winslow conservation wilderness.

### Tall Pines State Preserve
* **Location**: Bark Bridge Rd, Sewell/Deptford, NJ 08080 (15 min drive from Sicklerville)
* **Highlights**: 110-acre former golf course converted into South Jersey's first passive state preserve. Paved walking loops with minimal traffic, low crowd density, and calm natural scenery—ideal for individuals sensitive to auditory overload.

## 7. Local Police & Public Safety Programs

### Winslow Township Police Department
* **Emergency**: Dial `911`
* **Non-Emergency Dispatch**: `609-561-3300`
* **Police Records / Administrative**: `609-567-0700` (Ext 1100)
* **Location**: 125 South Route 73, Braddock, NJ 08037

### Camden County "Blue Envelope" Program
* **Purpose**: Enhances communication between police officers and autistic drivers during traffic stops.
* **How It Works**: Autistic drivers carry a distinctive blue envelope holding their driver's license, registration, insurance card, and emergency contact details. The exterior of the envelope provides clear tips for police officers (e.g. driver may display anxiety, avoid direct eye contact, or use repetitive speech).
* **How to Obtain**: Available free of charge at the Winslow Township Police Department lobby or the Camden County Store at Voorhees Town Center.

### Project Lifesaver (Camden County Sheriff's Office)
* **Contact**: `856-225-5470`
* **Purpose**: Provides radio-frequency tracking wristbands for children and adults with autism or developmental disabilities who have a documented history of wandering or elopement, enabling rapid recovery by search and rescue teams.
