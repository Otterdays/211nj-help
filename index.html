Quickstart checklist

  1. Call 211 for a full benefits screening (all programs at once) — 211 or 800-510-3102 · nj211.org
  2. Apply for USF / LIHEAP / LIHWAP — one application, three programs — energyassistance.nj.gov or call 211
  3. Apply for NJ FamilyCare — health insurance for all 3 of you — njfamilycare.dhs.state.nj.us
  4. Call SHARES — H2O water grant ($500) + emergency utility grants — 866-657-4273 · sharesnation.org
  5. Call DDD — get fiancée evaluated for Supports Program — 609-633-1482 · nj.gov/humanservices/ddd
  6. Apply for cash & child care — one app covers SNAP, WFNJ cash aid & child care — njhelps.gov or call 211
  7. Get free tax help — claim EITC / Child Tax Credit with free VITA prep — nj.gov/freefiling
Gather these first do this before any application
  • Photo ID + Social Security numbers for every person who applies
  • Proof of income — recent pay stubs and last 2 years of tax returns
  • Your deed or lease + a recent utility bill (proof of address in 08081)
  • Medical / disability records — autism & Asperger’s diagnoses, your back treatment, medication list
  • Birth certificates and the child’s school / IEP documents
  • Recent bank statements — a few programs (SSI, Charity Care) check assets

Keep copies in one folder — the same documents unlock almost every program below.

The marriage question

Be very careful before marrying for benefits. Marriage can raise household income limits for SNAP, Medicaid, LIHEAP, USF — but if either of you is on SSI, marriage can reduce or eliminate that benefit because spousal income counts. Married couples where both are disabled often do better than two individuals, but it depends on your specific income mix. Before deciding: call a benefits counselor through 211 or use the DCAid screening tool to model "what if I got married" scenarios.
